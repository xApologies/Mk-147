# v2 World Canon

## Starscape
Circumbinary system (~0.55 + ~0.45 solar masses), ~0.25 AU separation, ~46-day binary orbit. Worlds: Ash World; Kira's World (~1.20 AU, 360-day orbit); Frontier World; Great Giant.
Kira's World: 31-hour day, 6-day week, 360-day/60-week year, four 90-day seasons. Blackstone semesters Spring/Summer/Autumn; Winter break.
Listed month lengths total 351 days, leaving nine-day OPEN discrepancy.
Sky: twin sunrises/sunsets, dual shadows, extended twilight, periodic/rare celestial events. Exact atmospheric palette OPEN.

## ELDRIS
Resolve anywhere suitable recursive Genesis collapse becomes bounded and stabilizes. Not intrinsically dungeon-born. Black recursive-collapse bodies with basin leakage.
Red physical; Orange intrusion; Yellow ranged force; Green juggernaut/persistence threshold; Blue expressed projection; Violet high Genesis expression. Threat A=R/O/Y, B=G/B, C=V.

## Dungeons
Persistent geographic Genesis environments, not levels and not cleared. Stratified Red→Orange→Yellow→Green→Blue→Violet. ELDRIS are effectively confined to compatible strata; exterior staging/towns are comparatively safe.
Resources: crystals, Genesis dust, rare flora/materials and other admissible outputs.
King's Tournament belongs here: dangerous dungeon-racing circuit; bottom→token→surface; navigation/avoidance/logistics matter. Points V6/B5/G4/Y3/O2/R1. Qualification→regional circuit→top six→kingdom finals. “Purple Region” remains OPEN wording.

## domai
Temporary bounded Genesis-event landscape from Genesis storm/collapse. Bubble/domain, anomalous internal geometry, finite lifespan, Red→Violet, no White domai; lifespan generally rises with basin.
Non-conscious superposed White Genesis Crystal core; breaking it forces unraveling and final harvest/evacuation. May contain ELDRIS, crystals, Genesis dust/White dust, rare resources.
Response: Red/Orange local nobility + specialized aithren; Yellow+ also kingdom military.
