# Hostile Organizations

Current canon accepted in Increment 001 — 2026-10-02. Based on the supplied [canon snapshot](../07_PROVENANCE/increment-001/package/01_CANON/HOSTILE_ORGANIZATIONS.md) and the [explicit accompanying update request](../07_PROVENANCE/increment-001/EXPLICIT_UPDATE_REQUEST.txt). Genesis Point ancestry transfers selectively under the [authority addendum](../00_GOVERNANCE/INCREMENT_001_AUTHORITY.md).

## Pirates

Pirates are a major maritime criminal power. Organized piracy may include a Pirate King, major Pirate Lords/hubs, subordinate fleets, captains and crews, alongside independent pirates outside the primary hierarchy.

Core criminal activities include illicit drug trade, human trafficking/slave trade, smuggling, maritime extraction, and criminal logistics. Their organizational scope extends beyond isolated sea raids.

## Pirate Islands

Pirate Islands are continent-scale fragmented volcanic archipelagos: geologically unstable, volcanic, ash-heavy, tectonically active, and extraordinarily regenerative ecologically.

Environmental rhythm: Growth → Destruction → Regrowth → Repeat.

Pirates use these environments cyclically for staging, extraction, Genesis resources, lumber/resources, and maritime logistics. Geography, mobility, scale, and timing make conventional eradication or blockade extremely difficult.

Old permanent Domai Heart mechanics and Chimera ontology are not imported. Future Pirate-Island Genesis mechanics remain OPEN and must reconcile with the current finite-lifetime [domai model and ELDRIS ontology](../02_WORLD/V2_WORLD_CANON.md).

## Bandits

Bandits are localized terrestrial criminal/predatory groups. Typical activities include ambushes, road obstruction, night raids, cargo/supply theft, kidnapping, ransom, and hidden or relocatable camps.

They create travel insecurity and demand for caravan guards and [vaelum contracts](VAELUM_AND_ACCORD.md#operational-and-contract-doctrine). Old Sanctuary suppression cubes/rings are not imported.

## Cartels

Cartels are regional illicit-market and leverage networks, not sovereign powers or conquering armies. Methods may include dependency, vice, addictive substances, debt, financial entanglement, blackmail, kidnapping, trade/resource leverage, proxy actors, intermediaries, and deniable destabilization.

Their typical objectives are durable income, access, resource control, monopolies, debt leverage, and market leverage. They generally do not seek crowns, thrones, public legitimacy, or open sovereign rule.

Names, leaders, symbols, and exact territories remain OPEN.

## Cabal of Ash and Crown

The Cabal is a secret organization of maegi, scholars, and power-brokers. Accepted characteristics are secrecy, subterfuge, indirect control, long-horizon planning, institutional capture, succession/lineage manipulation as political strategy, cultural manipulation, historical manipulation, distributed clandestine operations, and century-scale strategic thinking.

These organizational characteristics do not establish Genesis Point supernatural abilities. Ancient longevity magic, destiny severance, prophecy manipulation, magical memory excision, bloodline magic, and other unreconciled supernatural abilities are not automatically imported. Any future capabilities require reconciliation with MK-147 Transduction.

Named members, hierarchy, sigils, and supernatural capabilities remain OPEN. See the [increment open ledger](../07_PROVENANCE/INCREMENT_001_OPEN_QUESTIONS.md).
