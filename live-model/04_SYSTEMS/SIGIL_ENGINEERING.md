# ru’ne, Sigils, Arrays, Industry

Accepted in Increment 002, 2026-10-02. `ru’ne` is the fundamental component of a sigil: a discrete physically inscribed, strongly orthogonal geometric element.

A sigil is the complete physically realized structure composed of ru’ne. It must exist on a substrate.

## Activation and persistence

Sigils are scale-independent and may be manual, continuous, or environmentally/conditionally activated. Small embedded sigils can provide mundane lighting and respond to ambient light.

Sigil persistence is principally material persistence. They can operate for extremely long periods while substrate and geometry remain intact; maintenance repairs physical damage/wear.

## Arrays and industry

A sigil array is an organized system of multiple sigils whose combined topology produces a coordinated system-scale effect.

Applications: water; sanitation/dissolution; lighting; barriers; industrial material growth; maege-glass production; metropolitan Genesis-field stabilization; prepared equipment effects.

Developed civilization can grow many materials through sigil-array industry rather than rely exclusively on mining/extraction. Sigil engineering is a major technological backbone and is predominantly infrastructural.

Blackstone's established Genesis-engineered architecture/constructs include mundane night lighting; extensive embedded sigil infrastructure can make the institution visually come alive after dark.
