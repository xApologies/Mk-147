`velis` — lowercase V-E-L-I-S. Author/workshop alias: **anchor**.

A `velis` is a localized, temporarily persistent topology formed directly within the Genesis Field by an Architect. It is not a physical sigil.

## Persistence and maintenance

Persistence depends on topological coherence. Heavy use can shorten life; stable low-load velis may persist decades or centuries. Interaction/use can loosen topology until it unravels into the ambient Genesis Field. Architects can maintain, reinforce, restore or replace a velis. No universal timer.

## Applications

Applications include agricultural velis that accelerate plant growth/nutrient uptake and defensive velis whose repeated activation creates an attritional maintenance problem.

Distinction: ru’ne = sigil component; sigil = complete physical ru’ne structure; sigil array = coordinated physical system; velis = Architect-created Genesis-field topology.
