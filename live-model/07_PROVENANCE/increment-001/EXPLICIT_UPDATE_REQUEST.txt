5. VAELUM
============================================================

Integrate `vaelum` as the organizational layer for aithren.

Core distinction:

aithren = individual/cultural participation identity

vaelum = persistent organization under which aithren may operate

A vaelum is:

- independent;
- formally recognized;
- contract-capable;
- not a Crown military unit;
- not a noble house;
- not itself a geographic place.

Aithren can work through a vaelum while remaining independent operators.

============================================================
6. ACCORD REGISTRY
============================================================

Integrate the Accord Registry as a Crown administrative domain.

Its current canonical purpose is simple:

The Accord Registry registers and formally recognizes vaelum.

Hierarchy:

Crown
→ Accord Registry
→ recognized vaelum
→ aithren members/teams

Do NOT import the older Genesis Point King's Highway architecture.

Do NOT invent broader Accord Registry authority.

The Registry currently establishes administrative recognition.

Other Crown domains may use that recognized status when applying lawful benefits.

7. VAELUM ECONOMIC RELATIONSHIP
============================================================

Integrate the distributed Crown incentive model.

The Crown benefits from maintaining decentralized civilian/aithren capability without putting all aithren on permanent government payroll.

Recognized vaelum may therefore receive qualifying economic benefits or tax reductions.

Examples already accepted:

- reduced taxation on qualifying harvested resources such as Genesis Dust recovered from ELDRIS;
- property-related reductions or credits for qualifying vaelum facilities/headquarters;
- housing/facility benefits associated with maintaining an operational organization.

Example:

A vaelum with approximately 200 members may own or lease a large headquarters/residential building and receive a modest qualifying property-related tax reduction.

DO NOT invent exact percentages.

DO NOT invent a complete tax code.

Exact rates and categories remain OPEN.

10. HOSTILE ORGANIZATIONS
============================================================

Create or update an appropriate Hostile Organizations domain.

Integrate the following organizational identities.

------------------------
PIRATES
------------------------

Major maritime criminal power.

Organized piracy may include:

- Pirate King;
- major Pirate Lords/hubs;
- subordinate fleets;
- captains and crews;
- independent pirates outside the primary hierarchy.

Core criminal activities include:

- illicit drug trade;
- human trafficking / slave trade;
- smuggling;
- maritime extraction;
- criminal logistics.

Pirates are not merely random sea raiders.

------------------------
PIRATE ISLANDS
------------------------

Transfer the geographic/worldbuilding concept selectively.

Pirate Islands are:

- continent-scale fragmented volcanic archipelagos;
- geologically unstable;
- volcanic;
- ash-heavy;
- tectonically active;
- extraordinarily regenerative ecologically.

Core environmental rhythm:

Growth
→ Destruction
→ Regrowth
→ Repeat

Pirates use these environments cyclically for:

- staging;
- extraction;
- Genesis resources;
- lumber/resources;
- maritime logistics.

Their geography, mobility, scale and timing make conventional eradication/blockade extremely difficult.

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT import the old Genesis Point permanent Domai Heart mechanics.

Do NOT import Chimera ontology.

Any future Pirate-Island Genesis mechanics must reconcile with the current MK-147 finite-lifetime `domai` model and current ELDRIS ontology.

------------------------
BANDITS
------------------------

Localized terrestrial criminal/predatory groups.

Typical activities:

- ambushes;
- road obstruction;
- night raids;
- cargo theft;
- supply theft;
- kidnapping;
- ransom;
- hidden/relocatable camps.

Bandits create travel insecurity and therefore create demand for caravan guards and vaelum contracts.

Do NOT import old Sanctuary suppression cubes/rings unless explicitly approved later.

------------------------
CARTELS
------------------------

Regional illicit-market and leverage networks.

They are not sovereign powers or conquering armies.

Methods may include:

- dependency;
- vice;
- addictive substances;
- debt;
- financial entanglement;
- blackmail;
- kidnapping;
- trade leverage;
- resource leverage;
- proxy actors;
- intermediaries;
- deniable destabilization.

Their objectives are typically:

- durable income;
- access;
- resource control;
- monopolies;
- debt leverage;
- market leverage.

They generally do NOT seek:

- crowns;
- thrones;
- public legitimacy;
- open sovereign rule.

Names, leaders, symbols and exact territories remain OPEN.

------------------------
CABAL OF ASH AND CROWN
------------------------

Secret organization of maegi, scholars and power-brokers.

Transferable organizational characteristics:

- secrecy;
- subterfuge;
- indirect control;
- long-horizon planning;
- institutional capture;
- succession/lineage manipulation as political strategy;
- cultural manipulation;
- historical manipulation;
- distributed clandestine operations;
- century-scale strategic thinking.

DO NOT automatically import Genesis Point supernatural mechanics such as:

- ancient longevity magic;
- destiny severance;
- prophecy manipulation;
- magical memory excision;
- bloodline magic;
- other unreconciled supernatural abilities.

Those require later reconciliation with MK-147's Transduction ontology.

Named members, hierarchy and sigils remain OPEN.
