# vaelum and Accord Registry
Canonical `vaelum` is lowercase V-A-E-L-U-M. A vaelum is a formally recognized independent organization under which `aithren` operate. `aithren` remains the person/cultural identity; vaelum is the persistent organization.

The Accord Registry is a Crown administrative domain that registers and formally recognizes vaelum. It does not command all aithren or make vaelum Crown military units.

Recognized vaelum form a decentralized private capability market. They fill temporary/specialist gaps for the Crown, nobles, cities, merchants, caravans, Blackstone/Institutions of Knowledge, researchers, physicians and private clients.

Operational envelope includes security, bandit suppression, caravan escort, temporary guard forces, dungeon escort, Genesis-storm/domai support, ELDRIS suppression, exploration, retrieval/recovery, harvesting, rare-material acquisition, investigation, dangerous transport, logistics, civil aid, and medical/agricultural/infrastructure support.

Research example: a researcher can hire a vaelum for a dungeon expedition; researcher owns the research objective while the vaelum supplies navigation, security, ELDRIS response, logistics, safe-work support, recovery and emergency extraction.

Crown incentives are distributed rather than one loot-tax rule. Recognized vaelum may receive qualifying tax reductions/credits on harvested resources such as ELDRIS-derived Genesis Dust and on organizational overhead such as headquarters/property/housing. Example: a ~200-member vaelum owning/leasing a large building may receive a modest property-related reduction. Exact rates/categories remain OPEN.

Contracts and reputation are valid concepts; exact quest-board/arbitration bureaucracy remains OPEN. King's Highway relationship and broader old Accord machinery are NOT imported yet.
