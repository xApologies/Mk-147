# `vaen`, Binding, Identity, PSSP, Fatigue
`vaen` — lowercase V-A-E-N — are naturally occurring specialty resources with unusual Genesis-relevant identities/properties, found in Wilds, dungeons, domai and other environments and harvested for Binding Circles, Alchemy and related uses.

Examples: Voidbloom Lotus; Teardrop of the Everlasting Star.

`vaen` replaces the fantasy-resource use of "elements". "Element" remains for Alchemical Type 1–5 materials.

A vaen can carry multiple identities. Binding can select one or more identities rather than transfer the entire source identity.

A Binding Circle is a large specialized sigil and identity-resolution scaffold. It selects admissible identities from identity-bearing inputs and collapses/resolves them into an occupied identity/state of a target. Targets can be people or objects.

Model: identity-bearing input(s) -> Binding Circle -> identity selection -> collapse/resolution -> target occupied identity.

Vaen are important identity-bearing resources but are not declared the only possible identity source.

For a person, Binding establishes the working concept currently called a **shaping**: the bound manifold/mechanism subsequently accessed/projected through PSSP. Final nomenclature for stored structure versus projected manifold remains OPEN. Binding and active shaping are separate stages.

Fatigue is physiological/endurance-like loss of the ability to sustain Genesis-field/basin access during shaping. It is not a mana bar. Recovery restores access. Training can improve endurance but cannot remove fatigue and does not automatically rewrite/upgrade the Binding.

Computer/RAM/CPU/runtime language is author-only analogy and must not become in-world terminology.
