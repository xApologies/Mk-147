# Genesis Storms and `domai`
A Genesis storm is a localized weather-like disturbance of the Genesis Field, creating unstable conditions with elevated potential for Genesis collapse.

A storm does not automatically become a domai. Storm-driven collapse can produce runaway/local collapse and ELDRIS without domai formation. A bounded environmental collapse can resolve into a domai.

`domai` — lowercase D-O-M-A-I — is a temporary bounded Genesis-event landscape; an environment, not a conscious organism.

Basin classes: Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Violet. No White domai. Lifespan generally increases with basin; exact timers undefined.

Domai have anomalous/non-isometric internal geometry. Nearby depth remains usable; at increasing distance ordinary depth information degrades into a blurred, approximately two-dimensional projection/surface-like horizon. Long-range distance/depth becomes unreliable. Exact visibility distance remains variable/undefined.

Structural core: non-conscious superposed White Genesis Crystal. It does not think, govern the domai or command ELDRIS.

Breaking/destroying the core forces unraveling. Natural expiration can also cause unraveling/dissipation. Core destruction is not instantaneous disappearance; unraveling is progressive and creates a final high-risk harvest/evacuation period.

Domai may contain ELDRIS, Genesis crystals, Genesis Dust including White Genesis Dust, `vaen`, and rare resources. ELDRIS are inhabitants/manifestations, not consciousness/core.

Generalized response doctrine: Red/Orange can involve local `vaelle` + specialized aithren; Yellow+ also kingdom military. Recognized vaelum can support storm/domai operations.

Do not import old Genesis Point permanent Domai Heart/Class-0 Chimera ontology.
