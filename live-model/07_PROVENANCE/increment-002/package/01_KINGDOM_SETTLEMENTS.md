# Kingdom Settlements
Four mapped territorial domains: Violet, Orange, Green, Blue. One capital per domain; Violet capital is also Kingdom Capital. Political classification remains OPEN.

Crown hierarchy: Capital City -> Large City -> Small City -> Village Hub.
Large City ~10x10 miles. Small City ~7x7 miles. Village Hub is a planned proto-city/civic nucleus; small-city-track core may begin ~0.5x0.5 mile. General model: ~60–80 years regional maturation plus ~10 years major redevelopment. Generalized figures, not universal timers. Hub persists as mature civic core.

Towns are organic traffic/economy-driven settlements, not a Crown hierarchy rung.

Hollowmere-type dungeon-support settlements are distinct from Village Hubs and absorb delver/aithren traffic, supply, lodging, staging and logistics. Green/Blue/Violet-reaching dungeons can become major regional economic anchors.

Cities can operate metropolitan sigil arrays stabilizing the Genesis Field over a much larger region. Large City example: five major sigils (four corners + center) as one array, potentially stabilizing on the order of a 200-mile radius. Example/generalized scale, not universal fixed radius.

Inside stabilized territory, agriculture, roads, estates, villages and `vaelle` holdings are viable because Genesis storms/collapse are suppressed. Outside reliable stabilization are the Wilds. Wilds describes Genesis-risk condition, not biome.
