Book 1:
Arc1 Afflicted Blacksmith → Binding Ceremony → extreme Binding Circle incompatibility → Violet basin binding → Nyx.
Arc2 aithren/adventure/team/Tournament; Chainmail Robe begins as practical blacksmith solution.
Arc3 robe spreads → Genesis Transductionist movement → accidental Kira fame.
Arc4 Blackstone becomes lived domain; helkir path; Day Zero/early Age of Knowledge.

Binding Circle incompatibility is a known minority phenomenon. Kira is an extreme living-singularity case; ordinary profession-targeted bindings fail and she exceptionally binds at basin level. Other incompatible people do not automatically gain basin binding.
